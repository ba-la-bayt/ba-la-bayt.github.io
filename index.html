WHO WE ARE

A community is built one evening at a time.

Ba-La-Bayt is a volunteer-run social space for the local Israeli and Jewish community in the Rockville area.

It's a place to catch up with old friends, meet new people, enjoy community activities, and bring ideas to life. The club is organized by members of the community, and everyone is encouraged to take part in whatever way they can.
